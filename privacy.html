Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026

TL;DR — Your job data and profile stay in your browser. We send job descriptions to MiniMax AI for evaluation. We don't sell your data. Ever.

1. Who We Are

Career-Ops is a browser-based AI job evaluation tool built and maintained by Gilang Yudhistyra Dalimunthe. You can reach us at gydalimunthe@usfca.edu.

2. What Data We Collect

When you use Career-Ops, we may collect:

  • Profile information — your career background, target roles, salary range, and visa status, stored locally in your browser's localStorage
  • Job descriptions — text you paste into the app, sent to MiniMax AI for evaluation
  • Account information (if you sign up) — your email address, stored securely via Supabase
  • Evaluation history (if logged in) — past job evaluations linked to your account

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To evaluate job descriptions against your profile using AI
  • To save your evaluation history so you can access it across devices
  • To improve the app experience

We do not use your data for advertising, sell it to third parties, or share it with anyone except the AI provider (MiniMax) for the sole purpose of processing your request.

4. Third-Party Services

  • MiniMax AI — processes job descriptions to generate evaluations. See MiniMax Privacy Policy
  • Supabase — stores account and evaluation data for logged-in users. See Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts — serves fonts. No personal data transmitted

5. Data Storage & Security

Profile data is stored in your browser's localStorage and never transmitted to our servers. Evaluation history (for logged-in users) is stored in Supabase with row-level security — only you can access your own data.

6. Your Rights

  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time
  • Export your evaluation history
  • Update or correct your profile information

To exercise these rights, email gydalimunthe@usfca.edu.

7. Cookies

We use only essential cookies for authentication (session tokens). We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Significant changes will be communicated via the app. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

Questions? Email gydalimunthe@usfca.edu
